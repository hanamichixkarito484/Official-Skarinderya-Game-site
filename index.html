What is Skarinderya?

Skarinderya is a warm, relaxing cooking simulation game. Chop, sizzle, plate, and serve as you grow from a humble food cart to a beloved neighborhood restaurant. Along the way, unlock new recipes, decorate your kitchen, and win over a cast of quirky, hungry customers.

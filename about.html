About Skarinderya

Skarinderya began as a small passion project between a handful of developers who love both cooking and cozy games. We wanted to create something that felt like a warm kitchen on a rainy afternoon — simple to pick up, endlessly satisfying, and full of charm.

Our Mission

We believe games can be relaxing without being boring. Every recipe, character, and kitchen upgrade in Skarinderya is designed to bring a small moment of comfort to your day.

Our Team

Development

A small team of engineers and designers building the game engine, art, and recipes.

Community

We listen closely to player feedback through our message box and community events.

Culinary Advisors

Real home cooks and chefs help make sure every dish feels authentic.

Meet Our Customers

Every player who visits your restaurant has a story. Here are a few fan favorites from the Skarinderya community.

KAPRE

A TOWERING GIANT WITH A SUPER SUPRISINGLY GENTLE HEART.

KAPRE IS A MASSIVE, DARK-SKINNED GIANT WHO DWELLS IN THE BRANCHES OF OLD BALETE AND ARCIA TREES, OFTEN SEEN SMOKING A GIANT TOBACCO PIPE. DESPITE HIS INTIMIDATING SIZE OF APPEARANCE, HE IS SUPRISINGLY CALM AND GOOD-NATURED – A GENTLE GIANT MOVES SLOWLY AND SPEAKS A LOW RUMBLE. HE HAS ENDLESS PATIENCE, MUCH LIKE THE ANCIENT TREES HE CALLS HOME. IN SKARINDERYA, THE KAPRE IS YOUR MOST RELAXED CUSTOMER. HE WILL WAIT AS LONG AS IT TAKES FOR HIS MEAL, PUFFING HIS PIPE CONTENTEDLY AS THE BIGGEST TABLE IN THE KARINDERYA

MANANANGGAL

A FEARSOME FLYER WITH ZERO TOLERAMCE FOR SLOW SERVICE.

THE MANANAGGAL IS A TERRIFYING CREATURE OF PHILIPPINE FOLKLORE – A SELF-SEGMENTING VAMPIRE WHO DETACHES HER UPPER BODY AT NIGHT AND TAKES FLIGHT ON ENORMOUS BAT-LIKE WINGS. SHARP-TONGUED AND EVEN SHARPER-CLAWED, SHE IS NOT ONE TO BE KEPT WAITING. SHE SWOOPS INTO YOUR KARINDERYA WITH A DRAMATIC ENTRANCE, FOLDS HER WINGS WITH AN IMPATIENT SNAP, AND TPAS THE TABLE ALMOST IMMEDIATELY.

DUWENDE

A TINY EARTH DWELLER WITH A BIG APPETITE FOR MISCHIEF AND TREATS".

DUWENDES ARE SMALL, GNOME-LIKE EARTH SPIRITS FOUND LIVING UNDER ROCKS, TERMITE MOUNDS, AND THE ROOTS OF OLD TREES. THEY ARE SHY BUT CURIOUS CREATURES, EASILY STARTLED BUT QUICK TO WARM UP ONCE THEY FEEL WELCOME. IN SKARINDERYA, THE DUWENDE TODDLES IN WITH CAUTIOUS LITTLE STEPS, OFTEN PEERING OVER THE COUNTERTOP WITH WIDE, HOPEFUL EYES. HE IS NOT AS IMPATIENT AS THE MANANANGGAL, BUT HE WILL QUIETLY DISAPPEAR IF IGNORED FOR TOO LONG. HIS SOUL DISH IS ARROZ CALDO — WARM, GINGERY, AND DEEPLY COMFORTING, JUST LIKE THE EARTHY BURROWS HE CALLS HOME. A HAPPY DUWENDE LEAVES A SMALL LUCKY CHARM BEHIND AS THANKS.

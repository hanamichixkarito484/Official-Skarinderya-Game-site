Meet Our Customers

Every player who visits your restaurant has a story. Here are a few fan favorites from the Skarinderya community.

🌳 Kapre

A gentle giant who lives in the old balete tree out back. He never orders less than a triple portion, and he tips in cigars — which the staff have learned to politely decline.

🦇 Manananggal

She only visits after dark, always alone, and always orders something with a lot of garlic — much to everyone's confusion, including hers.

🍄 Duwende

A small, mischievous regular who insists on saying "tabi tabi po" before entering. Treat him well and small good-luck charms tend to appear around the kitchen.
